## About IMAPSwap

IMAPSwap is an open-source tool developed by [StrapMail](https://www.strapmail.com/) to help you take back control of your emails.

It allows you to migrate emails between IMAP servers, giving you the freedom to choose where your emails are hosted and keeping control of your data.

**Your emails. Your data. Your freedom.**
